## 핵심 발견
- 시상하부 **AMPK가 ghrelin orexigenic 매개** (Lopez 2008 Cell Metab).
- AMPK가 **thyroid hormone energy balance 매개** (Lopez 2010 Nat Med).
- **Estradiol → AMPK → BAT thermogenesis** (Martínez de Morentin 2014 Cell Metab).
- **AMPK-ER stress-JNK1** axis가 thyroid hormones 작용 (Martínez-Sánchez 2017).
- **BMP8B → orexin AMPK → BAT** (Martins 2016 Cell Rep).
- **sEV-AMPKα1**: BAT activation → 비만 치료 (Milbank 2021 Nat Metab).
- **sEV-AMPKα2**: 뇌허혈 보호 (Ouro 2025).

## Framework
- 시상하부 AMPK = **whole-body energy balance canonical regulator** (Lopez 2016 Nat Rev Endocrinol).
- Fatty acid oxidation·ceramide·CPT1·malonyl-CoA 매개.
