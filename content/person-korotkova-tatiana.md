## 핵심 발견
- **LH oscillation organize feeding circuits**: gamma (30–90 Hz) gates food approach (Carus-Cadavieco 2017 Nature).
- **LH "transition cells" + beta oscillation** ~2초 전 행동 전환 (Chen 2024 Nat Neurosci).
- **LepR LH neurons** ★ — moderate hunger feeding 억제, social interaction 촉진 (Petzold 2023 Cell Metab).
- **LepR LH anti-anxiety** (Figge-Schlensok 2025 Nat Neurosci) — anorexia nervosa 회로 후보.
- **Orexin → ACC theta → prosocial behavior** (Kim JG 2026 Science).

## Framework
- LH = **3 motivational drive arbitration node** — hunger × safety × social.
- Multidimensional coding: neurochemical × oscillatory phase × connectivity × stimulus response.
