# Early-life programming of hypothalamic circuits by maternal obesity

## 한 줄 요약
임신 전·중·수유기 모체 obesogenic 환경이 자손 시상하부 회로 (특히 melanocortin·PVN·LH·kisspeptin)의 발달을 sex-dependent 방식으로 손상 → 비만·대사·생식·스트레스 dysregulation lifelong 위험. Quarta lab.

## 역학 / 임상

### 모체 비만 글로벌 현실
- 2050년까지 성인 obesity 3.8 billion (54%).
- 임신의 **16.3%가 obese**, 43.8%가 overweight/obese.
- Pre-pregnancy BMI ↑ → preeclampsia·GDM·preterm·C-section ↑.

### 자손 결과
- **대사**: 비만·인슐린저항·T2DM·CVD 위험 ↑.
- **내분비**: HPA·thyroid·생식 축 변화.
- **신경행동**: ADHD·ASD·인지 결손 위험 ↑.

### Birth weight U-shape
- Pre-pregnancy obesity → macrosomia (>90th) OR fetal growth restriction.
- Catch-up growth 후 child obesity 위험 ↑.
- Gestational weight gain이 modifier.

### GDM 단독 위험
- BMI와 독립적으로 child obesity ↑.
- Neonatal hypoglycemia → corticospinal injury · motor/cognitive 손상.

## DoHAD framework
- Pre-conception → lactation 어떤 단계든 lifelong 위험 결정.
- 모체 health: 식이질·정신스트레스·환경독소·내분비 disruptor.
- **Maternal undernutrition도 자손 비만 ↑** (Dutch Famine cohort) — both 끝이 위험.
- Epigenetics (DNA methylation·miRNA·histone)가 매개.

## 핵심 표적 회로
ARC melanocortin system (POMC vs AgRP/NPY + MC3R·MC4R)이 가장 sensitive. PVN·LH·kisspeptin 회로 동반 손상.

## 신경내분비 효과 (clinical + preclinical)

### 대사·체중
- Animal meta-analysis: 자손 weight·fat ↑ even on control diet (postnatal day 4 to 13 months).
- Lactation period가 sensitive window — 모체 60% fat (gestation) vs 45% fat (lactation) 둘 다 male 자손 phenotype.

### HPA 축
- 모체 obese → 11β-HSD2 (placenta) ↓ → cord cortisol ↓ + 자손 blunted cortisol 반응.
- Rodent: PVN CRH ↑, postnatal day 7 corticosterone ↑.

### Thyroid 축
- Maternal·neonatal T3 ↑ (인간); rodent에서 deiodinase ↓ in liver·thyroid·heart·hypothalamus.

### Reproductive 축
- 인간: 모체 BMI ↑·gestational weight ↑ → 딸·아들 모두 puberty 조숙.
- Rodent: Kiss1 hypermethylation, vaginal opening 지연 (female), testosterone ↓·LH ↑ (male).

## 6가지 pathophysiological 메커니즘

### 1. Neuroinflammation
- 모체 obese → 자손 ARC IL-6·IL-1β·TNF-α ↑.
- IL-6 → astrocyte proliferation (ARC·SON) — postnatal 지속.
- ARC가 BBB 약한 부위로 inflammation 표적.
- Melatonin이 obese dam 처치 시 NPY ↑·POMC ↓ 정상화.

### 2. Fatty acid · ER stress
- 모체 SFA → fetal 시상하부 통과.
- Newborn hypothalamic mean diffusivity ↑ → 5세 child fat ↑.
- ATF4·GRP78 ↑ in POMC·AgRP (P10).
- **TUDCA (chemical chaperone)** 처치가 POMC projection·leptin sensitivity 복원 (Park 2020) — 약물 path.
- ER stress ↔ autophagy 상호작용.

### 3. BBB · 신경혈관 발달
- 모체 obese → 자손 ARC BBB 투과성 ↑, capillary fenestration ↑.
- **Tanycyte vimentin ↓** in newborn → 형태 손상 (수가 아닌 morphology).
- 성체 cross-fostering to lean mother가 회복 — postnatal modifiable.
- Cerebrovascular: vessel density ↑, contractility 손상.

### 4. Leptin signaling ★
- **Postnatal leptin surge** (P0–P14 in mouse)이 ARC POMC·AgRP projection 발달의 핵심 trophic factor (Bouret 2004 Science).
- 모체 obese → leptin surge **blunted** → central leptin resistance + projection 손상 → 성체 비만.
- Cord blood leptin ↑가 child adiposity 예측 (9세까지).
- Adult ob/ob leptin 치료는 projection 복원 못 함 — **developmental window 결정적**.

### 5. Insulin signaling
- 모체 GDM·obese → fetal hyperinsulinemia.
- Insulin이 axon outgrowth · POMC projection 발달 필수.
- POMC InsR KO mouse: PVN connectivity·pancreatic vagal innervation 손상 (Vogt 2014 Cell).
- 모체 STZ-diabetes는 반대 효과 (POMC↑·NPY↑) — islet transplant·cross-foster 회복.

### 6. Epigenetics
- *Pomc* promoter **hypermethylation** in obese dam 자손 (rodent, stable into adulthood).
- 5hmC ↓ + 5mC ↑가 비만 예측.
- miR-505-5p ↑ in fetal hypothalamus → high-fat 식이 ↑ (maternal exercise가 정상화).
- Lechner 2023 — POMC epigenetic obesity risk variant + MC4R agonist 효과.

## 회로별 표적

### Melanocortin (ARC)
- NPY:POMC ratio ↑ in 자손.
- Ascl1 (proneural factor) ↓.
- POMC axonal outgrowth ↓ to PVN (Park 2020).
- POMC translatome reprogrammed (Haddad-Tóvolli 2020).

### LH circuits
- VMH(SF1/BDNF) → LH connectivity **강화** (정반대 방향).
- LH orexin leptin sensitivity ↑ (P16) → 과식 predisposition.
- BNST → LH glutamatergic synapse 강화 (Shrivastava 2023).

### PVN
- Oxytocin 뉴런 ↓ + NAc projection 손상 → 사회행동 결손.
- CRH 뉴런 ↑ but DMV projection ↓ → "stressed vagal" phenotype → gastric motility 손상.
- **PVN MC4R 회로가 hypertension 매개** (Samuelsson 2016 PNAS) ★.

### Kisspeptin
- Kiss1 ↓ via hypermethylation → 생식 축 손상.
- POMC progenitor가 Kiss·AgRP까지 분화 가능 — 모체 obese가 progenitor fate 영향.

### Autonomic
- 모체 obese → 자손 sympathetic WAT denervation (TH↓) → lipolysis 손상.
- Pancreatic parasympathetic innervation ↓.
- Fetal HRV ↓ (인간) → autonomic adaptability 손상.

### Non-neuronal
- Astrocyte 수 ↑ in ARC/SON.
- Microglia aberrant phenotype P16 (Mendoza-Romero 2025) — microglia 제거가 모체 obese-induced weight gain 차단.
- Mediobasal hypothalamic gliosis: GDM-exposed child (9–11세)에 관찰.

## Sex dimorphism
- Pre-pregnancy obesity → male 자손 body weight·adiposity 변화 우세.
- Insulin in chow-fed dam P4–16 → α-MSH·AgRP innervation **male only** 손상.
- *Pomc* hypermethylation = sex·postnatal diet에 따라 다름.
- *Agrp* epigenetic 변화 in male rat (Schellong 2020).
- Female이 chemogenetic POMC stress manipulation에 resilient (Fang 2023).
